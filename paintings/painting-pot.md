I painted this flower because it reminded me how beauty doesn’t need to travel far. Sometimes it grows quietly where it is. The small pot by the window captures that simple rhythm of everyday life: sunlight, water, patience. It’s a reminder that slowing down isn’t about doing nothing, but about noticing everything.
