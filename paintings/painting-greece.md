I painted this scene after longing for stillness, the kind that exists in quiet places by the sea. The girl sitting in front of the white house isn’t just resting; she’s pausing between thoughts, surrounded by sunlight and salt air. It’s a portrait of time standing still, of life lived gently, one breath at a time.
