The sun is setting, but the summer remains warm. Flamingos gather in the water, seemingly preparing to sleep or engaged in a discussion. To me, they all appear to be girls talking, their connection is like loving sisters. As the sun slowly descends behind the bushes, its rays transform from a bright yellow to a warm red. In this moment, the sisters and the sun merge, creating an unforgettable scene we can enjoy.
